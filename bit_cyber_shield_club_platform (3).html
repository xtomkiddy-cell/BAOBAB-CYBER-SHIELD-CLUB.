About BIT Cyber Shield Club

Founded at the Baobab Institute of Tanzania (BIT), BIT Cyber Shield Club provides practical security education, ethical hacking laboratories, and tech mentorship.

Our Mission

To empower students with hands-on technical skills, promoting ethical hacking principles and defense mechanisms.

Our Vision

To become East Africa's leading student cybersecurity initiative for tech innovation and digital defense.
